## Description

Pay Links gives the Nimiq community one link for the everyday money it already moves: thanking a forum helper, paying a translator, splitting a meetup cost or backing a group project. The link opens inside Nimiq Pay, ready to pay in two taps. No addresses to copy and paste.

## Builder story

The Nimiq community already sends small amounts to each other all the time: tips for forum helpers, thanks for translators and builders, shares of a meetup bill. But in Nimiq Pay that means copying an address into a chat, with no amount and no reason. I built Pay Links so anyone can share one link and get paid, and so every payment can also bring a new person into Nimiq Pay.
